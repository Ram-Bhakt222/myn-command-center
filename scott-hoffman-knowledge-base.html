Social & Marketing Strategy Dual-track

Scott Hoffman's blend: broad social-media reach feeding a B2B authority engine. Two tracks, one funnel — the Tribe builds proof and demand signal; the Authority track converts decision-makers.

Track A — B2B Authority

Channels: LinkedIn, YouTube, email, speaking.
Goal: credibility with hotel/HR/school buyers.
Content: outcome case studies, "participation→outcomes" POV pieces, ROI/retention data, walking-yoga-as-Trojan-Horse explainers, IAYT/therapy authority.
Cadence: 2–3 LinkedIn posts/wk, 1 long-form YouTube or article/wk.
KPI: qualified inbound demos, anchor-account meetings.

Track B — Broad Tribe

Channels: Instagram, TikTok, Reels/Shorts.
Goal: reach + demand proof + talent/instructor pull.
Content: walking-yoga clips, "yoga for people who hate yoga," 60-sec accessible-movement demos, sleep/anxiety hooks, behind-the-scenes.
Cadence: 4–7 short videos/wk, ride the +2,400% walking-yoga search wave.
KPI: reach, saves, email captures into nurture.

Scott's Frameworks Consulting notes

Core operating frameworks from Scott Hoffman's consulting, applied to MYN.
  • Trojan Horse format: lead with the lowest-barrier offer (walking yoga) to clear the buyer's "my people won't do yoga" objection, then expand into deeper therapeutic programming once trust is established.
  • Dual-track marketing: Broad Tribe (IG/TikTok) generates reach and demand proof; B2B Authority (LinkedIn/YouTube) converts decision-makers. One funnel, two motions.
  • Outcomes over participation: sell measured results (sleep, anxiety, retention, attendance), not class counts — the only durable moat against $20–250/class commodity vendors.
  • Anchor-first sequencing: win hotels as anchor references, run schools in parallel, take corporate selectively, keep individual as funnel leverage.
  • Authority compounding: every pilot becomes a case study becomes authority content becomes the next pilot — proof flywheel.
  • "Space to think" standard: strip any subject line, claim, or CTA a serious medical reviewer would flag. Optimize for the buyer's decision quality, not vanity conversion.
