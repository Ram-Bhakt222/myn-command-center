3. School District Wellness Coordinator

Pain Points (refreshed May 18): Student mental-health crisis, compliance requirements, teacher burnout; wellness programs are first-in-line for lean-budget cuts. FUNDING UNLOCKS CONFIRMED THIS CYCLE: (1) Title IV ESSA grants explicitly cover yoga, mindfulness, and SEL initiatives; districts receiving >$30K must allocate ≥20% to "safe & healthy students" with another ≥20% to "well-rounded education" — yoga therapy qualifies for both categories. (2) CYBHI: California Department of Health Care Services is distributing $65M across 58 County Offices of Education for TK-12 mindfulness, resilience, and well-being programs. (3) Calming Kids and similar foundations offering $250-$1,000 program grants. Priority: Evidence-based programming; yoga/mindfulness proven for coping, attention, SEL, academics, and reducing bullying / disciplinary referrals. Budget: Federal flex bucket + state CYBHI dollars + foundation grants stack. Decision Criteria: Compliance fit (ESSA + state), measurable student outcomes, teacher bandwidth preservation, framing as cost-saving (absenteeism ↓, turnover ↓).

4. Healthcare/Clinic Administrator

Pain Points (refreshed May 18): Patient outcomes, practitioner coordination, insurance reimbursement complexity. Yoga therapy integration accelerating: Casey Health Institute incorporating yoga-therapy clinical outcomes into EMR — building cost-effectiveness evidence base for reimbursement (PubMed). Coverage today is uneven: yoga therapy led by licensed therapist may be covered; HRA/FSA/HSA reimbursement available with physician recommendation; some Medicare Advantage plans cover yoga via fitness benefits. Physical therapists with yoga therapy certification may bill under PT benefits (YogaMedics precedent). Priority: Integration with clinical workflows + measurable health metrics + credentialed delivery. Budget: Variable; tied to therapy services + benefit design. Decision Criteria: Clinical outcomes, licensing compliance, patient satisfaction, billing-code alignment.
