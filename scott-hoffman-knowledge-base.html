2. Corporate HR Director

Pain Points (refreshed June 1): Wellness budgets defended against rising healthcare costs; HR teams under pressure for measurable healthcare-cost offset, sick-day reduction, retention impact. NEW emphasis this cycle: Wellbeing is now C-suite-owned and measured like any other financial driver — CEOs are tying wellness directly to financial results, retention, and productivity (Wellhub 2026). Priority: Hybrid-ready programs (now baseline), whole-person coverage, integrated mental-health stack, preventive mental fitness, on-demand access. 91% Gen Z = non-negotiable; 95% workers view wellbeing as interconnected; 74% Gen Z = on-demand mental health is #1. Budget: ~$742/employee/year avg; ROI: $1.50-$3 per $1 — but ROI proof is now a gate, not a tiebreaker. Decision Criteria: Cost-offset modeling, monthly impact reporting, engagement with segmentation, C-suite-readable scorecard, integration with existing mental-health vendors.

3. School District Wellness Coordinator

Pain Points (refreshed June 1): Student mental-health crisis, federal compliance requirements, teacher burnout; wellness programs are first-in-line for lean-budget cuts. Funding stack confirmed: (1) Federal mandate: every local education agency in federal Child Nutrition Programs must implement a wellness policy — nutrition + physical activity + student wellness. (2) Title IV ESSA grants explicitly cover yoga, mindfulness, SEL; districts >$30K must allocate ≥20% to "safe & healthy students" and ≥20% to "well-rounded education." (3) CYBHI: California Department of Health Care Services distributing $65M across 58 County Offices of Education for TK-12 mindfulness/resilience programs. (4) State-specific: Rhode Island requires school staff wellness programs (substance abuse prevention, health assessments, physical activity). (5) Calming Kids and similar foundations offering $250-$1,000 program grants. NEW staff-wellness angle: Districts increasingly include yoga + mindfulness in employee wellness programs — teacher retention is now a measured outcome (NASBE). Priority: Evidence-based programming; yoga/mindfulness proven for coping, attention, SEL, academics, reducing bullying / disciplinary referrals; teacher-retention measurement. Budget: Federal flex bucket + state CYBHI dollars + foundation grants stack. Decision Criteria: Compliance fit (ESSA + state), measurable student + teacher outcomes, teacher bandwidth preservation, framing as cost-saving (absenteeism ↓, turnover ↓).

4. Healthcare/Clinic Administrator

Pain Points (refreshed June 1): Patient outcomes, practitioner coordination, insurance reimbursement complexity. Yoga therapy integration continuing to accelerate: Casey Health Institute incorporating yoga-therapy clinical outcomes into EMR — building cost-effectiveness evidence base for reimbursement. Coverage is uneven: yoga therapy led by licensed therapist may be covered; HRA/FSA/HSA reimbursement available with physician recommendation; some Medicare Advantage plans cover yoga via fitness benefits. Physical therapists with yoga therapy certification may bill under PT benefits (YogaMedics precedent). Priority: Integration with clinical workflows + measurable health metrics + credentialed delivery. Budget: Variable; tied to therapy services + benefit design. Decision Criteria: Clinical outcomes, licensing compliance, patient satisfaction, billing-code alignment.
